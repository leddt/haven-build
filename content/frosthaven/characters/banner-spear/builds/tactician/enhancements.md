# Enhancements

You’ll notice that very few of our formations have the option to enhance. That’s fine \- the attacks are already overtuned given how difficult they are to actually eprform. What we really need is better movement. My first choice would be a (\+1) on move with Pincer Movement, so we can pick up more loot and enhance more rapidly. Next, we need to jump as frequently as possible. Jump on Tip of the Spear is our best bet. We can also pick up \+1 movement on Boldening Blow if we find ourself rich enough. If you are in a small party, I recommend an extra move for the Reinforcement. That will enable many more formations that require more precise positioning. After that, we can enhance some of our more powerful formations. A \+1 on meat grinder is expensive but powerful. If we find ourself drowning in money, adding a second target to Rallying Cry becomes absurdly powerful. Being able to disarm two enemies at once suddenly makes this action worth keeping around.
