# Starting Items

Assuming you’re starting with only the starting purchasable items, I would always select jump boots as my opening item. It is absolutely essential that you be able to reach the enemy backline when necessary, in order to pull off some of the more challenging formations. That leaves you with 15 gold. Personally I prefer to buy a hand item here; the head and chest items are fine for us but we’ll be able to craft replacements fairly quickly. Poison dagger and heater shield are both fine options, depending on whether you think you want tankiness insurance.
