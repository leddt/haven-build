These are our cards that we will bring to every single scenario, until we gain a few levels and find stronger replacements. We’ll be sorting cards into three major categories: formations, movement, and utility. Some cards will have the flexibility to be used in multiple roles; as these arise, we’ll mark these in pink. 

We’ll be bringing every card with a formation on it, but only [Rallying Cry](#rallying-cry) should be used for its top action every cycle. We prefer to use the top of [Resolved Courage](#resolved-courage) over the bottom, but sometimes you just need to move two very quickly, so we’re ok using that one for initiative alone. We’ll consider each of the other formations, particularly [Tip of the Spear](#tip-of-the-spear), when the situation calls for it, but the other cards have key alternate uses that we need to emphasize. 

We need a few large move options to put ourself into position for our formations. [Set for the Charge](#set-for-the-charge) is a fantastic choice, and while we’d prefer to use [Tip of the Spear](#tip-of-the-spear) as a top action, sometimes we just need more movement. [Combined Effort](#combined-effort) provides a great way to put allies into slightly more efficient positions, and provides a simple backup formation for when we don’t think we’ll be able to pull off anything fancier. 

The formation on the top of [Unbreakable Wall](#unbreakable-wall) is great, but much of the time it’s more important to gain the permanent shield. If we’re shooting for formations such as [Pincer Movement](#pincer-movement) and [Resolved Courage](#resolved-courage) that put us in risky situations, we’re going to need to be able to tank a hit or two. I would say that unless a significant amount of the enemies pierce, it’s more important to get this shield than it is to have another decent formation. [Pincer Movement](#pincer-movement) is our best single target damage option but also our only loot at any level, so we’ll keep it around as long as possible in order to ensure that we can rebuild Frosthaven to its full potential.
