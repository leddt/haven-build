Boldening Blow has a nice top action, but it’s not really enough damage that it needs to sit permanently as a formation. We’re more excited about the bottom action, which will enable most of our formations very easily. Just as with [Combined Effort](#combined-effort), this action is much less exciting if the party is small. I would take the falcon instead if we have a two player party, and rely on both the falcon and the [Reinforcement](#at-all-costs) to formations whenever possible. In that case, I would cut [Regroup](#regroup) or [Pincer Movement](#pincer-movement) to make space.
