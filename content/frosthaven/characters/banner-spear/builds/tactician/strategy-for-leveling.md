# Strategy for Leveling

It’s tempting to simply take every formation that comes up. We’ll be doing that for the first few levels, but we need to ensure that we have the initiatives and movement to guarantee our formations will work. We’ll be looking to have 3\-4 good movements at all times, perhaps 1\-2 utility cards, and to fill the rest of our hand with strong formation options. If a card provides a formation plus movement or utility, it’s a must\-have.
