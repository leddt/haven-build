Meat Grinder is a very strong formation. It does put us in a bit of danger, but the damage potential is one of our strongest yet, and if we don’t feel safe we have the bottom to fall back on. We drop [Javelin](#javelin) as we’re hoping not to need a ranged attack; however, if you’re in a large and crowded party, I would drop [At All Costs](#at-all-costs) instead, since we won’t need to rely on that summon as frequently. Without [Javelin](#javelin) we have less actions that we’re excited to move with, so we’ll be looking at either [Tip of the Spear](#tip-of-the-spear) or Meat Grinder as our third key movement option.
