The tactician build works to get the most out of the Bannerspear’s formations. This requires the Bannerspear to be quite mobile, in order to reach the locations required for some of the more complex formations. As a result, we won’t be working to carry any banners with us from room to room, since we cannot afford the bottom actions required to put banners in the right places, nor can we rely on being able to block them from taking hits. However, we will emphasize some of our tankier actions, to enable us to move to the riskier locations required by our formation attacks. 

There are a few reason why you might prefer the Tactician build for Bannerspear:

1. You enjoy the movement and positioning puzzle that Frosthaven offers. The tactician will need to carefully position themself and their summons in order to enable the most powerful formations. 
2. You feel comfortable coordinating over multiple rounds with your allies. You can react to their natural positioning, but you will be strongest if you encourage them to move into spots that enable your attacks, for this round and the next. 
3. You want to maximize the damage that you deal with your own actions. This is the Bannerspear build that pulls the most attacks from their own attack deck. 

If your party has these things, the Tactician build will fit well:  


- Melee allies. You can accomplish the formations using Reinforcement and other summons, but you will be much more successful if you have melee allies who can fill the blue spots for you. Also, while this build can tank some damage, it can’t tank *all* the damage, so you’ll want at least one other character who is comfortable taking a hit now and again. 
- Not too many melee allies. If you are running in a party with melee Drifter, melee Deathwalker, and a skeleton swarm Boneshaper, you are going to find that the spots you need are frequently occupied. Summons are a particular concern as their movement is much more difficult to control. That’s not to say that you should avoid this build if you have a Boneshaper in your party, but you will need to be aware that you might not be able to perform as many maximal formations as you would if they were focusing on one large summon over a swarm of weak ones. In my experience, 3\-4 melee allies is the best number for finding allies for your formations without overcrowding.
