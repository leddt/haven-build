We need three additional cards to round out our hand. We’ll immediately cut out [Deflecting Maneuver](#_hu136o2o5ou), as we don’t really need more fast initiatives, we don’t want another bottom action that doesn’t enable formations, and we don’t expect to make enough wind to justify the top. [Incendiary Throw](#_8o6bxqg6p44y) also doesn’t make the cut, as we won’t have bonus damage frequently enough to guarantee the wound with our poor modifier deck. We only need to cut one more card, and usually I would choose [Driving Inspiration](#_yc7xrq7k6p0v). [Javelin](#_yjv3bbxgjo2l) hits a bit harder, we’ll be close enough to the action that the range two attack (assuming [Unbreakable Wall](#_u2k00e745ao6)) isn’t a huge liabliity, and has a bottom action that we can use to enable formations. That being said, if someone on your team benefits heavily from the light, you can consider bringing [Driving Inspiration](#_yc7xrq7k6p0v) instead. I would always consider [At All Costs](#_b47um9ulhbgb), even in crowded parties, because it will often enable a formation when your own positioning is already perfect, and because it will tank one hit and provide a strong, reliable source of XP. Finally, [Regroup](#_f2fhrv8ka4t7) provides one additional quick\-ish initiative, as well as a very strong action to perform between rooms. We can use the bottom of [Regroup](#_f2fhrv8ka4t7) to reposition allies for formations but I found that 25 initiative isn’t reliable enough to guarantee that the enemies haven’t repositioned themselves, so it’s better to rely on something like combined effort to secure formations.
