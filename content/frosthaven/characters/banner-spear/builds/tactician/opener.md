Unlike some classes, we don’t have a lot we need to do in order to set up. We can drop the bottom of [Unbreakable Wall](#_u2k00e745ao6) to open the scenario, but that’s a bottom action and doesn’t cost us our valuable top. I would run this action every scenario for at least 2\-3 levels, unless there is enough pierce on enemies that you think it won’t be useful. [Set for the Charge](#_fheghtjp3um9) is a strong opening play if you expect a good chunk of the enemies in the room to attack; try to position yourself in the most aggressive spot, so you take the bulk of the opening volley. If you anticipate that at least enemies might not run up next to you, perhaps because they are ranged, or because the opening positions are awkward, you can swap out this action with [Javelin](#_yjv3bbxgjo2l), using it before [Unbreakable Wall](#_u2k00e745ao6) to attack with the full three range. After this, you will need to run your formations based on where the enemies end up. In the best case scenario, I would try to run something like the above plan. Positioning ourself and an ally, we can hit two targets with [Tip of the Spear](#_9biaxul0blo7). The following turn, we move quickly and hit some targets with [Resolved Courage](#_zgu5oklkgem7), backing up using a basic move on whichever card we thinking might be the least valuable. This means that we’ll be attacking for a decent amount and immobilizing at least one enemy, most likely two or three. If we could choose the locations for our enemies, this would be the optimal plan. Of course, they might not be lined up in a perfect shape to hit the right number of targets, and the immobilize might not be incredibly useful, so you will absolutely need to react to both the enemies and your positioning.
