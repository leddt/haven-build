# Perks

![](../../images/character-sheet.png)

- [ ] <!-- check:rest-movement --> Before we do anything else, we need to grant our banner the extra two movement each rest. It hurts to delay improving our AMD, but try for the “bring the banner with you” mastery on the first scenario and feel how much just a little extra movement will accomplish. It’ll be painful, but we should be able to get that mastery right away, giving us the jump start of this perk.
- [ ] <!-- check:variable-plus-ones --> The main benefit of this build is playing around crowded frontlines. This means we should be hitting enemies that are surrounded frequently enough to validate replacing our 0s with the variable \+1s. If we’re lucky, these will average out to somewhere between \+2 and \+3, which makes this an above average perk.
- [ ] <!-- check:replace-minus-ones --> Even though we don’t plan on being near the frontline or wearing armor, removing \-1s is still a great use of our time. We’ll first replace them with rolling shields, then ignore item effects.
- [ ] <!-- check:add-modifier-cards --> We don’t really benefit much from the remaining non\-AMDs, so we’ll improve our deck by adding cards. I’d usually recommend adding the rolling \+1s first, then the disarms, then the push cards, but you can prioritize or delay the disarm depending on how much CC your party needs. In any case, we aren’t taking enough damage to justify the healing modifiers until the end.
- [ ] <!-- check:remaining-non-amd --> The other two non\-AMDs aren’t huge improvements for us \- we probably aren’t opening doors, and we aren’t trying to take hits. Pick these up at the very end, in whichever order you’re the most excited about.
