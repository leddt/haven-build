# Summary

The Breezing Banner Build is the closest we’ll get to support as a Bannerspear. Standing back, we’ll throw out ranged attacks, healing, and support our team with Banners. As the name “breezing” implies, we’ll be making good use of wind in order to accomplish our goals. We won’t deal or absorb as much damage directly as the other builds might, but we’ll do a fantastic job at enabling our team to do what they do best. 

There are a few reason why you might prefer the Breezing Banner build for Bannerspear:

1. You don’t like putting yourself in consistent danger. The Bannerspear is tanky, and we’ll still want to use our healthpool, but tanking isn’t our main focus. We won’t frequently delve into enemy territory and we won’t plan to spend entire rounds tanking up, so we’ll be sharing the brunt of damage mitigation with the rest of our team. 
2. You enjoy playing with elements. Out of all the builds, this is the one that most heavily emphasis infusing and consuming wind for value. 
3. You enjoy enabling your team. You’ll deal your fair share of damage, but your main job is to stay out of the way and let your allies deal damage and tank, while you support them with healing, banners, and conditions. 
4. You are excited by the banner mechanic. This build will run a banner from the beginning of most scenarios, and eventually attempt to run two. 

If your party has these things, the Breezing Banner build will fit well:  


- A huge amount of melee allies. Unlike the other Bannerspear builds, we’ll be standing back for the majority of the fight. We can occasionally dip in for a melee attack, and we’ll have a summon here and there, but for the most part we can get by without ever taking up a valuable frontline slot.
- A huge amount of allies in general. A part of what validates this build is the banners that grant bonuses to all allies within a certain range. The more of these allies there are, the more powerful your bonuses will be. Your best friend, as always, is Boneshaper; here, the best Boneshaper is one that summons a skeleton at every opportunity. 
- A well\-rounded party. You don’t fill any one role well enough that your party can give up on a certain necessary trait. Make sure your party is able to deal damage, tank some hits, and provide CC if you weren’t there.
