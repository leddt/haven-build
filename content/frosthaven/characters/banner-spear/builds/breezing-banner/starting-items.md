# Starting Items

We’ve got a few options here, but I’d typically go with the Weighted Net. You’re wasting a bit of your starting cash, but adding immobilize to an attack will frequently mitigate a ton of damage. You could also go the heater shield and poison dagger \- you won’t be in melee range frequently, but they certainly aren’t going to waste overall. However, it’s unlikely that the rest of your party isn’t interested in these items, so let others have first dibs on everything but the net.
