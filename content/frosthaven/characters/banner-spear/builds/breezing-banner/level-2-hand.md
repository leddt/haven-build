Pinning Charge gives us a fourth ranged attack, allowing us to more reliably deal damage. We will drop [Resolved Courage](#_zgu5oklkgem7), and only use [Incendiary Throw](#_8o6bxqg6p44y) if there’s an unshielded enemy to target; if not, we try to lose it on our first long rest. If you have an ally that is making frequent AOE attacks, drop [Incendiary Throw](#_8o6bxqg6p44y) instead and keep [Resolved Courage](#_zgu5oklkgem7) around to boost their power. Now that we have an outlet for wind, be sure to line up [Javelin](#_yjv3bbxgjo2l) into Pinning Shot whenever possible. We’ll get more cards that provide wind soon, giving us additional opportunities to capitalize on it.
