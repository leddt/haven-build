These are our cards that we will bring to every single scenario, until we gain a few levels and find stronger replacements. We’ll be sorting cards into four major categories: banner(s), attacks, movement, and utility. Some cards will have the flexibility to be used in multiple roles; as these arise, we’ll mark these in pink. 

Our main banner will remain for all nine levels as [Banner of Strength](#_oncl1tu76ylp). Adding \+1 to each ally’s attack each turn is insanely strong, and gets stronger the more allies you have. At early levels, you’ll probably have trouble carrying this through the entire scenario, so try not to summon it until the second room if it looks like there will be a huge amount of walking to do. 

Our movement for the banner will mostly come from [Combined Effort](#_bj30gaanrifd) at the start. We’ve got [Regroup](#_f2fhrv8ka4t7) as well in a pinch, although we love the top of that card as well. We’ll work to pick up some extra granted movement as soon as possible. For ourself, [Set for the Charge](#_fheghtjp3um9) provides excellent movement and excellent initiative. 

For attacks, we have two very strong options in [Javelin](#_yjv3bbxgjo2l) and [Driving Inspiration](#_yc7xrq7k6p0v). Remember: you are an ally to your banner, so these are actually attacks for four and three once you’ve got it online. Also, we can consider dropping the [Banner of Hope](#_yc7xrq7k6p0v) down instead of or in addition to the [Banner of Strength](#_oncl1tu76ylp) for scenarios where enemies have innate wound, poison, or brittle. We’ll have trouble bringing both at earlier levels, but once we’ve picked up a few perks and a few level up cards, we can easily consider both banners. 

Finally, we’ll pick up some strong utility. We can use either half of [At All Costs](#_b47um9ulhbgb); if it’s crowded, then the top is likely to do a ton of work. Heal up your team, then regenerate yourself with [Regroup](#_f2fhrv8ka4t7) and you’ll be able to make use of your large health pool without ever taking a hit. The bottom might be harder to use if the frontline is crowded, but you can still tank a large instance of damage, run over a trap, or else contribute in other meaningful ways.
