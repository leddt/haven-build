# Opener

We’ll start by listing out an example of what your first three turns might look like when starting a scenario. The action outlined is the one we’ll use for initiative.

| Bottom Action | ![](../../images/rallying-cry.png) | ![](../../images/resolved-courage.png) | ![](../../images/at-all-costs.png) |

We’re making the assumption here that we feel confident getting our banner from room to room. It’s going to be ambitious to do this right from the getgo, so if it seems like it’ll be difficult I recommend waiting until you’ve rested twice and have entered the second room, which will buy you additional turns as well as save you valuable movement actions. Once you have a single level or a few perks, we should have no issues doing this. 

On the first turn, we’ll set up our banner and chuck out a ranged attack. Either attack will do here; [Javelin](#javelin) is probably better since it’s unlikely we’re getting a kill here, so high damage is more important than consistent damage. 

The second turn we’re lining up our wound. Ideally we have an unshielded enemy in a clump of shielded ones, but it’s unlikely we’re able to properly pick and choose our targets so precisely. Still, this line means the attack is for three damage, meaning that even against an enemy with one shield we’re wounding on a (\-1), which gives us great odds to succeed. 

From here, it’s whatever your team needs. We can heal a bit with [Regroup](#regroup), heal a lot with [At All Costs](#at-all-costs), or simply attack again. On bottom, we can start moving ourself and our banner if we hae space; if not, consider making the [Reinforcement](#at-all-costs) to tank a hit from one of the remaining enemies.
