Barricade pushes out [Deflecting Maneuver](#deflecting-maneuver) as a quick attack that increases our survivability for a round. I would bring back [Deflecting Maneuver](#deflecting-maneuver) for the bottom in scenarios that have an extreme number of ranged\-only enemies, and I would drop [Regroup](#regroup) to make room. If you are in a two player party, or else the scenario has a large number of heavy hitters rather than a swarm of weaklings, consider setting up the banner on Barricade. In this instance, we’ll use [Let them Come](#level-03) as a formation attack, since we can’t really afford three losses, and again give up [Regroup](#regroup).
