We’re two cards short of a full build. Attacking isn’t our top priority but there will be turns when things are close enough to death that we’ll want to attack, and turns where we don’t have a tanking card because there simply aren’t enough of them at level one. Our final options are [Resolved Courage](#resolved-courage), [Rallying Cry](#rallying-cry), [Javelin](#javelin), [Driving Inspiration](#driving-inspiration), and [Incendiary Throw](#incendiary-throw). [Resolved Courage](#resolved-courage) actually makes a lot of sense for us: if we run up and stand in front, enemies should clump around us in this formation anyway. We’ll be able to pick up a few good hits and back up to prevent lots of damage. We’ll pick up [Javelin](#javelin) for now. There will be lots of scenarios where the two shield on [Deflecting Maneuver](#deflecting-maneuver) doesn’t do anything, at which point we’ll want to use the top. Of course, we’d like wind for the top, and so we bring [Javelin](#javelin). The short range on [Javelin](#javelin) will hurt us but if there aren’t ranged enemies, then odds are there’s a melee enemy to hit after backing up by a hex. [Javelin](#javelin) also works great as utility, pulling enemies through traps or else simply closer to you, so that you can properly tank. That being said, don’t be afraid to pick up [Driving Inspiration](#driving-inspiration) and drop the banner down if a scenario has enemies that apply wound, brittle, or poison on every attack. It’s not really worth the effort unless you’re blocking these frequent conditions, but if you are then the banner absolutely holds its own.
