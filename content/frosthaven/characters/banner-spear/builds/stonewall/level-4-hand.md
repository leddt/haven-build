Now that we have two granted moves that also move ourself, we move [Regroup](#regroup) back to utility. This leaves us enough top actions that we probably won’t run [Resolved Courage](#resolved-courage) as frequently, though we should still bring it in scenarios that are extraordinarily melee\-heavy, if we need another very fast initiative, or if we have allies that are getting phenomenal use out of the bottom.
