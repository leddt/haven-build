# Starting Items

Assuming you’re starting with only the starting purchasable items, you’re going to want a heater shield for sure. This will allow you to tank as heavily as possible. The other tankiness items are fine but we’re going to quickly replace them with craftable options, so I would spend our other fifteen gold on a poison dagger. We’ll replace that once we find a better shield, but there’s nothing craftable that we’re excited for at low prosperity, so we can use the poison dagger on our infrequent attacks for additional value.
