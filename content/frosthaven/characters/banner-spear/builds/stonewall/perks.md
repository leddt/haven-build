# Perks

1. For obvious reasons, the two shield non\-AMD is fantastic for us. It isn’t good enough that we’ll want to rush it, but if we ever get two perks at the same time, we should pick this up. If we don’t have it after we’ve gotten our first 4\-5 regular perks, we should come back and take this one normally. ![](../../images/image25.png)
2. We won’t be attacking as much as other builds, but AMD perks are still important for us. We’ll ignore negative item effects ASAP so that we can wear armor. We should buy the armor before we get this perk if the opportunity arises, the (\-1) isn’t bad enough that it offsets the extra survivability of even the early armor options. 
3. The long rest perk for granted movement is essential by level three, in order to help our banners keep pace. It does very little for us until then, so I recommend taking this with the perk gained at level three regardless of what you already have. 
4. The rolling shield modifiers will end up being excellent for us. If we’re lucky, we can pull one of them with a quick attack now and again and mitigate even more damage. 
5. Rolling Heals will similarly help keep us alive. 
6. Now that we’ve lined up the most valuable AMD perks, we can pick up the extra movement when we open a door. Most of the time, we actually want to use this to run back, rather than in. The best way to mitigate damage is to make sure nobody gets hit. 
7. There aren’t a lot of other cards we particularly care about \- they are all decent value, they just don’t synergize exceptionally with what we’re trying to accomplish. I’d prioritize the disarms first, then the rolling cards, then the pushes, and finally replacing the \+0s with what will usually be \+1s and occasionally \+2s.
