Utility

![h=20rem](../../images/pincer-movement.png)

The banner on Let Them Come will be our secondary source of tankiness. With that and [Unbreakable Wall](#unbreakable-wall), we’ll be able to get a permanent two shield as long as we are careful with our positioning. We drop [At All Costs](#at-all-costs), since we need more bottom actions for moving ourself and our banner and can’t really afford to take a turn off to drop the [Reinforcement](#at-all-costs). Use [Regroup](#regroup) to move your banner ahead once you have the time \- it’ll be slow, but it’s probably worth it. 

It’s worth noting that delaying one of the losses until after our first rest buys us an extra round due to how the stamina works out. If you think that stamina, rather than survivability, is the limiting factor for a scenario, consider delaying the banner until you’ve finished your long rest. Not only will it buy you an extra turn, but it will also save you the hassle of moving the banner around if you can move yourself near to the next door before you rest. As we don’t have much granted movement yet, this could end up being a huge deal.
