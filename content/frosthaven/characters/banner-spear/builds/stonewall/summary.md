# Summary

The Stonewall build focuses on being the tankiest tank of all time. Your plan is to stand in front of your team and make sure that nobody, including you, will ever die. The crux of the build involves combining [Unbreakable Wall](#unbreakable-wall) with the banner on [Let Them Come](#level-03) to have two permanent shield. You will still provide some attacks and some healing, but ultimately your main goal is to ensure that neither you nor your banner will die, so that your team can focus all of their efforts on attacking. 

There are a few reason why you might prefer the Stonewall build for Bannerspear:

1. You don’t need to be the hero with damage. Your contribution to the team will be just as strong as your allies, but it won’t frequently be through flipping your AMD to damage enemies. Instead, you’ll absorb and heal damage while granting additional movement to allies. That’s not to say that you’ll never attack, but it won’t be your main focus. 
2. You don’t like when bad enemy flips ruin a scenario. You’re always preparing for the worst case scenario with this build, so it’ll be quite rare that an unlucky enemy flip will ruin your life. 
3. You aren’t rushing a personal quest from level one. This build really comes into its own at level three, so if you think you’re going to be able to retire in less than ten scenarios, you’ll probably only have about half your lifespan to really engage with the tanking tools. If you really want to run this build and you’re starting a fresh campaign, pick one of the personal quests that you can’t really rush down. Your party will be very grateful that you took the time to complete one of these. Besides, tanking builds are most effective when the tank is the highest level in the party, so that they’re tanking enemies weaker than the class was expecting to see. 

If your party has these things, the Stonewall build will fit well:  


- No dedicated tank. Even in a 4 player party, once you hit level three it’s likely you will be able to handle all damage mitigation for your team. If the rest of your team is unable or unwilling to take hits, you will fill in quite nicely. 
- Heavy damage and crowd control. Since you aren’t frequently looking to focus on attacking, you shouldn’t expect to deal much damage to enemies, and it’s unlikely that you’ll spend much time disabling them. Your team will need to be able to make good use of the space you make for them with impressive damage and CC. 
- Allies who are able to stay out of the way. You’re able to tank up, but you aren’t really mobile enough to jump to the perfect place to tank from since you need to lug a banner behind you and protect it. As a result, if your allies are going to be entering dangerous territory you won’t be able to protect them well. Ranged allies and allies that can initiative weave will both be excellent allies.
