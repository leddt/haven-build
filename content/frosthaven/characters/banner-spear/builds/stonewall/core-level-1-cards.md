These are our cards that we will bring to every single scenario, until we gain a few levels and find stronger replacements. We’ll be sorting cards into three major categories: formations, movement, and utility. Some cards will have the flexibility to be used in multiple roles; as these arise, we’ll mark these in pink. 

Our core focus is survivability. We’re bringing [Unbreakable Wall](#unbreakable-wall) for obvious reasons, but [At All Costs](#at-all-costs) and [Deflecting Maneuver](#deflecting-maneuver) are additional options we’re excited about. [At All Costs](#at-all-costs) doesn’t make us tankier directly, but the Reinforcement will run ahead and soak up one large hit. We’re better at soaking frequent small hits, so use this for when an enemy is planning to hit hard.

We need movement to get from place to place. Our positioning doesn’t need to be precise, as we aren’t running many formations, and those that we are running won’t put us in awkward positions. Still, two move fours will get us to where we need to be, and tanky builds put special emphasis on fast initiatives. [Combined Effort](#combined-effort) can be used to help our allies keep up, and will be especially crucial for once we bring a banner into play. 

We bring [Pincer Movement](#pincer-movement) exclusively for the loot. We don’t ever plan to use the top action. [Regroup](#regroup) has some excellent healing which is stronger between rooms due to the regenerate; we can use it during a fight, but it will be much more impressive if we use it as we walk from one room to the next, healing for 5\-6 instead of just for 2.
