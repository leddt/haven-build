# Perks

Use this as a checklist while leveling. Shared across builds — your build page may call out which perks to prioritize.

1. Remove two `-1` cards
2. Replace one `-1` with one `+1`
3. Add one `+1` ROLLING Strengthen
4. Add one `+2` ROLLING Shield 1
5. Ignore item negative effects and add one `+1`
