# Level 5

![](../images/explosive-epicenter.png)![](../images/hold-the-line.png)

Explosive Epicenter is a lot less impressive than it looks. Getting yourself *and* an ally into position between two lines of enemies just won’t happen. This can easily hit three things and you’ll get more than three at *best* once in a scenario, unless you’re putting yourself in undue harm to prove me wrong. Either there won’t be space to stand behind the melee line or the ranged line will be too far back. Still, [Unbreakable Wall](#unbreakable-wall) is a solid and reliable card that deals with retaliate, so having a second copy is fine. Plus, we might want to run the shield loss on [Unbreakable Wall](#unbreakable-wall), so this lets us keep the top formation while tanking up. The little movement boost can be nice to retreat from the enemies or pursue them, depending on what’s needed. 

The bottom loss here, on the other hand, is much *more* impressive than it looks. Being able to reposition, even slightly, without needing a bottom action to do it, will *vastly* improve our power level. This won’t work between rooms, but it should still provide enough effort during a fight that we’re happy to have it. We can more easily bring banners with us and position them precisely during battles. We can wiggle ourself and our allies to line up a formation. A *very* significant benefit from this card is that the movement can happen at any point on your turn, so this can be before or after any other movement you use or grant. Being able to shift in, hit, and then run out makes the initiative dance a bit more of an initiative cakewalk. Of course, if we’re running this and another loss, like a banner, that’s already two losses on a ten card class, which is a lot. We will need to watch our stamina. If we can afford the hit, then this card will find absurd value in any build. 

Hold the Line’s top action will set us up with “tank the whole room” levels of shield. Pair this with [Unbreakable Wall](#unbreakable-wall) and you’ll have three shield for the turn starting at initiative five. Four if we have the shield banner up. Six if you also run the bottom of deflecting maneuver. Eight if you pop the perk for shield. Of course, this requires two lost cards and a once per scenario perk and only works on ranged attacks \- but for that one round, you are *invincible*. More realistically, you are going to have this plus one of the other two shield effects for three shield \- and most of the time, that’s enough. You also splash the shield to your friends \- you’d rather tank it all yourself, but multitarget effects happen and you can’t plan for everything, so we definitely won’t complain. 

The bottom action gives some movement, some shield, and some retaliate. It’s great for busting down a door if your goal is to run into the room rather than break the door than run away. Whether it’s worth a loss is questionable. Sometimes you run into a room and there are lots of ranged enemies who won’t get retaliated on, or else some non\-attack turns meaning even the shield is wasted. Normally you can just cancel the card and run a move two, but that won’t work if you’re running into a closed room. When you aren’t bursting into a room, the bottom can be good but it’s very hard to predict when. Still, there’s absolutely nothing wrong with move 2 at initiative five. 

[Tactician](#tactician/summary): Explosive Epicenter is one of our most important cards. The top provides a strong formation, and the bottom gives us a reliable way to set up some of the other ones. 

[Stonewall](#stonewall/summary): Hold the Line is the clear choice, giving us additional tankiness at breakneck speeds. 

[Breezing Banner](#breezing-banner/summary): Neither of these really appeal to us. We’d enjoy the bottom of Explosive Epicenter if we could spare the stamina, but we really can’t. As a result, we’re going back for [Boldening Blow](#level-04). This will make it easier to bring our banner along, let us move allies to swarm for more powerful [Pinning Charge](#level-02) hits, and overall provide strong bonus movement for our team.
