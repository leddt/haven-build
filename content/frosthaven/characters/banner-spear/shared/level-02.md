Meat Grinder is a *nice* formation. The pattern is in theory most similar to [Rallying Cry](#rallying-cry), but in practice most similar to [Pincer Movement](#pincer-movement) in where you need to be to get this off optimally. To ping one enemy, you and your ally can stand side by side; if that’s your plan, you probably should have just brought [Combined Effort](#combined-effort) for similar damage and an easier pattern. If you’re looking to get two targets it probably means you are jumping into the fray. If you can hit both enemies, then you are rewarded with a lot of damage and two wounds. The initiative would be nicer if it was a bit slower or a lot faster, so try to ensure that you line this one up to get in and out safely. 

The bottom is *very* good. Three move is solid, and a heal one will frequently turn into a heal 5 with the regeneration. This one is usually best used out of combat, to make sure the regeneration finds some value, but is worth using in a fight if there are conditions to deal with, or if the target of the heal can avoid getting hit for 1\-2 rounds. 

The strength of Pinning Charge’s top attack will depend on your party composition. In a typical two player party, this is at *best* an attack three immobilize. That’s fine but nothing worth building around, and the attack is all but worthless without wind. In a four player party with boneshaper, this could theoretically be an attack eight, and will often be an attack five. That’s much more impressive, and we can combine the this with a bottom granted movement to ensure that we get closer to the ideal number. The immobilize is less useful in this instance, since the enemy is surrounded at what is likely a fairly quick initiative, but if it’s a ranged enemy we at least get some disadvantaged attacks out of it. 

The bottom action is similar to [Regroup](#regroup). It has a bit longer range and moves three allies instead of two. The versions of granted movement that don’t move ourself are still usually less impressive; even a move one would be nice, to adjust slightly based on the chaos of battle. Still, this is a net gain of six movement over using a move two on ourself, so if we are trying to catch up between rooms this is fairly efficient. Pair that with the fact that our banners don’t move and we have some move fours, and you might find more value in this than at first glance. 

[Tactician](#tactician/summary): Meat Grinder gives us a powerful (albiet risky) formation, and an exceptional bottom action. We’ll happily pick it up. 

[Stonewall](#stonewall/summary): The heal on Meat Grinder is just what we’re looking for. 

[Breezing Banner](#breezing-banner/summary): The top of Pinning Charge will be extremely powerful if our allies are being swarmed, and we can use the bottom until we find better options for moving our banners.
