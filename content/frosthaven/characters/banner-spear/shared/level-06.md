# Level 6

Barricade is an *awesome* card. Unlike [Meat Grinder](#_1wdqzivm9ia1), we don’t have to put someone in undue danger to hit multiple targets with wound here. We do need the help of two allies instead of one, but the positioning is fairly safe and we’re going fast enough that we’re likely the one taking the damage here. Even if we aren’t, we gift a shield to everyone involved, meaning that the damage will be reduced before it’s shared. Don’t expect to easily hit four targets with this every time, but three is very realistic and even two is a fine use of our time. 

Banner of Valor can be great or terrible, depending on what you’re fighting. Against some bosses, this is almost cheating. One large hit a turn reduced to zero means you don’t really have to worry about much of anything. Against enemies with frequent small hits, swarms of weak enemies, or damage over time such as wound, this is mediocre at best. Also, this only blocks one hit, not one hit per ally, making it somewhat stronger at lower player counts, contrary to the trend from our other banners. Don’t summon this one at the start of each scenario like you might the [Banner of Strength](#_oncl1tu76ylp). Instead, decide whether or not it does something useful, like the [Banner of Hope](#_yc7xrq7k6p0v), and act accordingly. 

Bolstering Shout’s top action is very unimpressive compared to [Regroup](#_f2fhrv8ka4t7) \- one more heal is underwhelming after five more levels. That is, unless we have wind, at which point it’s more like triple the healing. Another fairly straightforward action, it provides something great to do between rooms and puts some additional emphasis on wind. 

The bottom action is a clean upgrade to [Javelin](#_yjv3bbxgjo2l), although we might miss the quick initiative. We move a fair bit further, and we pull twice the enemies. The improvements here are very significant, allowing us to set up more ambitious formations. For instance, pairing this with [Explosive Epicenter](#_vcsc412wsl00) could actually validate hitting more than three targets if an ally is already in position. Overall, there isn’t a lot of new things to say about this card because it’s just better versions of two cards we already love taped together. 

[Tactician](#_916yt4oqmln1): Barricade is an excellent formation for us, as long as we have the allies to position for it. It’ll be our pick as long as we’re confident we can pull this off at least 2\-3 times within a scenario. If not, then consider Bolstering Shout as an upgrade to Regroup, or going back for Boldening Blow if you picked up Air Support at 4 in a small party. 

[Stonewall](#_ydaqocq9xs69): We really want both of these cards. We’re coming back next level for whichever one we’ve left behind here, so pick whichever option you think is the most interesting. I would usually pick Barricade first, since we don’t have extremely reliable control over wind, but both options are great. 

[Breezing Banner](#_rggu3f79bw1o): Bolstering Shout is our best use yet for wind. It’s exactly the kind of support action we’ve been looking for.
